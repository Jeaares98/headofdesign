# Design Research Source Registry

Use this registry to guide research. Sources are starting points, not authorities. Always evaluate relevance, recency, evidence quality, and applicability to the actual product.

## Product and interaction excellence
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Apple product experiences
- App Store editorial / notable apps
- Google Material guidance where platform-relevant
- platform-native design documentation

## Web and visual craft
- Awwwards
- 21st.dev
- CodePen
- Supahero
- leading digital product studios and product design case studies

## Gaming and engagement systems
Study leading games and live-service products for mechanisms, not visual copying. Relevant lenses include onboarding, progression, mastery, feedback, rewards, personalization, social identity, collection systems, seasonal/live content, and comeback loops.

Example research targets may include Fortnite and other category-leading games when relevant to the product problem.

## Cultural adjacency
Look beyond conventional SaaS references when the audience requires it:
- fashion
- music
- sport
- entertainment
- creator products
- community platforms
- media
- retail

## Competitive research
Always include:
- direct competitors
- adjacent substitutes
- products solving the same human need through a different category

## Source evaluation
For each source ask:
- Is it current enough for this question?
- Is the pattern actually used in production or only a concept?
- Is there evidence of user impact?
- Is it relevant to this audience and context?
- Are we observing the interface or assuming why it works?

## Output rule
Never return a list of links as the final research product. Convert references into mechanisms, product implications, and testable design decisions.
