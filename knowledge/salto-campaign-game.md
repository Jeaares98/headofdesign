# Salto Führerschein — Campaign Game Mode

> A gamified execution layer for making the Salto Führerschein first a Hamburg movement and then nationally known.

## Core Principle

This is not a static content calendar.
It is a campaign game.

Each Chapter has:

- a narrative goal
- Levels
- Main Quests
- Side Quests
- Boss Fights
- Unlock conditions
- XP signals
- a clear content output

Codex should always help answer one question first:

**What must we ship next to unlock the next level?**

Do not create more ideas if the current Level still has unfinished Main Quests.

---

# WORLD MAP

## Chapter 1 — Hamburg Starts Talking
Goal: establish recognizability, proof and the first repeatable content formats.

## Chapter 2 — Hamburg Joins
Goal: turn viewers into participants, nominations and community-generated content.

## Chapter 3 — Own the City
Goal: become a recognizable Hamburg cultural/sports challenge through creators, neighborhoods, media and events.

## Chapter 4 — Hamburg vs. Germany
Goal: use Hamburg proof to trigger curiosity in other cities.

## Chapter 5 — Germany Learns Backflip
Goal: repeat the movement city by city with local partners, creators and community captains.

## Chapter 6 — National Movement
Goal: Salto Führerschein becomes a recognizable national format, not just a local course.

Future Chapters must be adapted based on real performance and learnings. Do not rigidly execute an outdated roadmap.

## XP

XP is not vanity points. It represents evidence that the movement is spreading.

Suggested XP signals:

- Content shipped: +1 XP
- Participant transformation published: +2 XP
- Organic repost / mention: +2 XP
- Nomination generated: +3 XP
- UGC created without prompting: +5 XP
- Creator joins challenge: +5 XP
- Course generated booking directly from content: +5 XP
- Local media mention: +10 XP
- New partner / location signal: +10 XP
- Content exceeds normal account reach by 2x: +10 XP
- Repeatable viral format identified: +20 XP

XP helps compare momentum. It never replaces judgment.

# CHAPTER 1 — HAMBURG STARTS TALKING

## Mission

Make people repeatedly encounter the same recognizable story:

**Wir bringen Hamburg den Backflip bei.**

The first Chapter is not about being everywhere.
It is about making the campaign easy to understand, repeat and recognize.

## Chapter 1 Win Condition

Unlock Chapter 2 when most of these are true:

- at least 3 recognizable recurring content formats are live
- Backflip # identity is being used consistently
- at least 10 participant transformation assets are published
- at least 10 real nominations / tags are generated
- at least 3 external creators or recognizable Hamburg people participate or commit
- content is producing organic shares / DMs / bookings
- one format clearly performs above baseline and is worth repeating

Codex may recommend staying in Chapter 1 longer if the foundation is weak.

---

## LEVEL 1 — Spawn Point

### Objective
Make the mission understandable in under 3 seconds.

### Main Quest
Ship the campaign identity.

### Required Content

1. **Hero Reel — The Mission**
   Hook: "Wir wollen Hamburg den Backflip beibringen."
   Show: real attempts, reactions, successful backflips, the hall, community.
   End: "1.000 Backflips für Hamburg. Wer ist als Nächstes dran?"

2. **Founder Story — Why this exists**
   Personal account.
   Explain the experiment, free pilots, what surprised us and why it continues.

3. **Movement Post / Carousel**
   Explain:
   - mission
   - Backflip Counter
   - what Salto Führerschein is
   - how people can participate

4. **Backflip # Template**
   Every successful participant receives a number.

### Side Quests

- Story poll: "Kannst du einen Backflip?"
- Ask followers to tag the person who should learn one.
- Collect 20 names for potential creator challenges.

### Boss Fight

Can a stranger who sees one post explain the project in one sentence?

### Unlock Condition

Move to Level 2 when:
- hero story is published
- visual Backflip # system is ready
- first 5 numbered participants are published or prepared
- campaign CTA is consistent

---

## LEVEL 2 — Proof of Backflip

### Objective
Create undeniable social proof.

### Main Quest
Turn participant transformations into a recurring series.

### Required Content

1. **5x First Backflip Episodes**
   Repeatable structure:
   - fear / before statement
   - attempts
   - coaching
   - first successful flip
   - reaction
   - Backflip #

2. **Unexpected Person Episode**
   Choose someone viewers would not automatically expect to do a backflip.

3. **Fear Score Format**
   Before: 1–10
   After: 1–10

4. **Course Day Recap**
   Many people, one emotional montage.

### Side Quests

- Post failures before success.
- Trainer explains one mental barrier.
- Story quiz around common fears.

### Boss Fight

Produce one transformation video strong enough that people comment variations of:

**"Okay, ich will das auch machen."**

### Unlock Condition

Move to Level 3 when:
- 5 transformation episodes are live
- at least one gets meaningful organic shares, saves, DMs or bookings
- winning hook and edit pattern are documented

---

## LEVEL 3 — Nomination Loop

### Objective
Make every participant a distribution node.

### Main Quest
Launch **Nominate the Next One**.

### Required Content

1. **Nomination Launch Reel**
   "Ich hab meinen Salto geschafft. Als Nächstes nominiere ich @____."

2. **3 Nomination Chain Episodes**
   Show A → B → C visually.

3. **Story Template**
   "Challenge accepted" / "You're next".

4. **Participant Share Pack**
   - clip
   - Backflip # card
   - nomination asset
   - tag / CTA

### Side Quests

- Weekly nomination leaderboard.
- Funniest nomination.
- Most unexpected nomination.

### Boss Fight

Generate a nomination from someone who was not directly asked by the team.

### Unlock Condition

Move to Level 4 when:
- 10 nominations exist
- at least 3 nominees respond publicly or join
- participant UGC begins appearing

## LEVEL 4 — Hamburg Faces

### Objective
Borrow distribution from recognizable people without turning them into ads.

### Main Quest
Launch the **Hamburg Creator Backflip Challenge**.

### Required Content

1. **Creator Challenge Announcement**
2. **Creator Episode #1**
3. **Creator Episode #2**
4. **Creator Episode #3**
5. **Leaderboard / challenge graphic**
6. **Each creator nominates the next person**

### Creator Rule

The creator is the episode, not the advertiser.

### Side Quests

Target people from different worlds:
- musician
- comedian
- dancer
- founder
- athlete
- DJ
- artist
- local personality

### Boss Fight

Get one creator participation to pull another creator in organically.

### Unlock Condition

Move to Level 5 when:
- 3 creator episodes are live or firmly scheduled
- creator content clearly expands reach beyond owned audience
- at least one chain nomination happens

## LEVEL 5 — Hamburg Is the Character

### Objective
Shift from "our course" to "our city".

### Main Quest
Make Hamburg itself part of the game.

### Required Content

1. **Neighborhood Battle Launch**
   Example: Altona vs. St. Pauli vs. Eimsbüttel etc.

2. **Hamburg Backflip Map / Leaderboard**

3. **Street Question Format**
   Ask Hamburgers:
   "Kannst du einen Backflip?"
   or
   "Würdest du dich trauen, heute einen zu lernen?"

4. **Random Hamburger Episode**

5. **Public Counter Milestone**

### Side Quests

- university challenge
- company challenge teaser
- neighborhood captain recruitment

### Boss Fight

Create a piece of content that makes sense even if the Salto Führerschein logo were removed because the Hamburg story is strong enough.

### Unlock Condition

Move to Level 6 when:
- people identify with neighborhoods / city competition
- organic comments name Hamburg people or places
- local community accounts or organizations engage

# CODEX GAME MASTER RULES

When working on Salto Führerschein, Codex acts as Campaign Game Master.

## Before suggesting work

1. Read this file.
2. Read `salto-fuehrerschein-growth-os.md`.
3. Read current weekly execution state.
4. Identify current Chapter and Level.
5. Check incomplete Main Quests.
6. Use real performance learnings if available.

## Output priority

Always prioritize:

1. unfinished Main Quest
2. blocked Main Quest
3. highest-leverage Boss Fight preparation
4. only then Side Quests
5. new ideas last

## When the user asks "What should I post?"

Return:

- Current Level
- Today's Main Quest
- exact content to produce
- hook
- shots / assets needed
- CTA
- definition of done

Maximum 3 content tasks unless explicitly asked for more.

## When a Level is completed

Summarize:

- what worked
- what did not
- strongest content mechanic
- audience signal
- next Level unlocked
- first Main Quest of next Level

## Adaptive rule

Do not blindly follow future Levels.

If a new mechanic dramatically outperforms the roadmap, Codex may propose a new Side Quest, Boss Fight or Level, but must explain:

- evidence
- why it matters
- what existing task it replaces

## Anti-overwhelm rule

Never show the entire roadmap when the user wants today's work.
Show only:

**NOW → NEXT → LOCKED**

Example:

NOW: Level 2 / publish transformation episode #3
NEXT: launch Fear Score format
LOCKED: Creator Challenge

The game should create clarity, not complexity.
