# Design Research Skill

## Purpose
Build a living design-intelligence layer from best-in-class products, contemporary interaction patterns, award-winning apps, gaming UX, and emerging web techniques.

## Research sources
Prioritize:
- official product sites and release notes
- App Store / Google Play editorial selections and award winners
- Awwwards and comparable curated showcases
- 21st.dev and modern component ecosystems
- CodePen experiments for interaction and animation techniques
- Supahero and contemporary product/visual references
- leading consumer apps
- gaming products with exceptional onboarding, retention, progression, social, and feedback systems

## Research format
For every useful reference record:
- product/reference
- date observed
- category
- pattern/mechanism
- why it works
- user emotion
- likely implementation approach
- where it could fit
- risks/limitations

## Rules
- Prefer mechanisms over screenshots.
- Separate enduring patterns from temporary visual trends.
- Never recommend a trend merely because it is popular.
- Verify contemporary examples when the task depends on what is current.
- Use primary sources where possible for product behavior and technical capabilities.

## Monthly benchmark
Maintain a shortlist of outstanding current products and experiences worth studying. Evaluate each on:
- UX clarity
- interaction craft
- visual identity
- motion
- accessibility
- onboarding
- retention mechanics
- originality
- technical execution

The shortlist should influence future design decisions only when the underlying mechanism fits the product problem.
