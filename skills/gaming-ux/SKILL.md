# Gaming UX Skill

## Purpose
Translate proven game-design mechanisms into non-game digital products without creating manipulative or childish experiences.

## Analyze these mechanisms
- onboarding and first-time-user experience
- goals and quests
- visible progress
- mastery and competence
- feedback loops
- rewards
- streaks and continuity
- collections
- status and identity
- personalization
- social proof and cooperative behavior
- challenge calibration
- anticipation and surprise
- comeback mechanics
- loss aversion safeguards

## Reference mindset
Study successful games such as Fortnite and other best-in-class titles for interaction and motivation patterns, but never copy their visual identity by default.

For every borrowed mechanism explain:
1. Original behavior
2. Psychological/user need it serves
3. Product-relevant adaptation
4. Risk or ethical concern
5. Measurement approach

## Guardrails
Do not use:
- deceptive scarcity
- gambling-like mechanics where inappropriate
- intentionally confusing cancellation
- punishment loops designed primarily to create anxiety
- fake social proof

The goal is voluntary engagement through clarity, progress, competence, identity, and delight.
